# 90-second demo: After Hours

1. Show the evening food plaza in 3D. Walk with WASD and point out the 18 stalls.
2. Click Rani’s pani puri stall and ask what makes her recipe special.
3. Say, “I like spicy food, but I’m exploring with my little sister, so she needs something mild.”
4. Visit the dosa or idli stall. Ask for a recommendation for both of you. The LLM can use the earlier conversation and saved preference in the new reply.
5. Hold Space, ask the vendor a follow-up, and release. The recognized speech appears as a player message and the vendor reply is read aloud.
6. Show the memory panel and refresh the page to demonstrate that the conversation and useful details persist.

The vendor biographies and menu are authored context; replies and memory extraction are generated by the configured LLM.
