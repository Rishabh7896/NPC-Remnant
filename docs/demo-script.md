# 90-second demo: After Hours

1. Start the evening in the full-window 3D food plaza and point out the 18 staffed counters.
2. Type or say, “Take me to the masala dosa stall.” Show the LLM recognizing the destination and the avatar walking there.
3. Ask Lakshmi what she recommends, then share a preference such as, “I like spicy food, but I’m exploring with my little sister, so she needs something mild.”
4. Say, “Find something we’ll both like.” The LLM uses the conversation and saved details to choose a destination and continue the scene.
5. Hold Space for a follow-up and release. The vendor reply is read aloud; the stall conversation and shared memories persist.

The vendor biographies and menu are authored context; replies and memory extraction are generated by the configured LLM.
